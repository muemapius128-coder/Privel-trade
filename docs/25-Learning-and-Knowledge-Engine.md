# Overview

The Learning and Knowledge Engine is the long-term intelligence system of the Privel Trade platform.

Its purpose is to continuously capture, organize, validate, and apply knowledge gathered from trading activities, market observations, research, AI reasoning, user interactions, historical performance, and external financial information.

Unlike conventional trading platforms that remain static after deployment, Privel Trade evolves continuously by learning from experience while maintaining governance, security, explainability, and operational control.

---

# Vision

The Learning and Knowledge Engine enables Privel Trade to become an adaptive financial intelligence platform capable of improving its analytical capabilities through structured learning rather than manual rule updates.

The engine supports:

- Continuous learning
- Knowledge accumulation
- Institutional memory
- Strategy evolution
- AI improvement
- Explainable intelligence
- Long-term adaptation
