# Overview

The Market Intelligence Engine is the analytical core of the Privel Trade platform. It continuously collects, processes, correlates, and interprets market information from multiple financial domains to produce actionable intelligence for traders, investors, institutions, and AI agents.

Unlike traditional trading systems that rely on isolated indicators, the engine combines technical analysis, fundamental analysis, macroeconomic intelligence, market microstructure, liquidity analysis, sentiment analysis, machine learning, and cross-asset relationships into a unified decision-support framework.

The Market Intelligence Engine transforms raw financial data into contextual insights, enabling informed decision-making while reducing uncertainty.
