# Overview

The Compliance and Regulatory Framework defines how Privel Trade identifies, implements, monitors, and continuously improves compliance with applicable legal, regulatory, contractual, and internal governance requirements.

As an AI-powered financial intelligence platform, Privel Trade operates in an environment where security, privacy, transparency, auditability, and responsible AI are essential.

This framework establishes the policies, controls, responsibilities, and monitoring processes required to support trustworthy and compliant platform operations.

---

# Vision

Privel Trade aims to maintain a compliance program that supports innovation while protecting users, financial data, platform integrity, and regulatory obligations.

The framework enables:

- Responsible AI
- Secure operations

- Continuous compliance
