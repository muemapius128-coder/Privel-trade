# Overview

The Product Roadmap defines the strategic evolution of the Privel Trade platform across multiple development phases.

The roadmap provides a structured vision for transforming Privel Trade from an AI-powered trading platform into a globally distributed financial intelligence ecosystem supporting individual traders, enterprises, institutions, and autonomous AI services.

The roadmap serves as a planning guide rather than a fixed delivery schedule. Priorities may evolve based on customer feedback, technology advances, regulatory developments, and business strategy.
