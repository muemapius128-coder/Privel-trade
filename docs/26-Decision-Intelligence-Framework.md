# Overview

The Decision Intelligence Framework is the cognitive layer of the Privel Trade platform.

Its purpose is to transform observations, analytics, predictions, research, and knowledge into structured, explainable, risk-aware decisions.

Rather than relying on isolated trading signals or individual AI models, the framework combines intelligence from multiple platform components to evaluate opportunities, assess uncertainty, prioritize alternatives, and recommend the most appropriate course of action.

Every significant platform decision passes through this framework before execution.

---
